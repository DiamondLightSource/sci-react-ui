Checks by mode

ΔE is CIEDE2000 under Machado et al. (2009) at full severity. The core needs CVD ΔE or more and every pair ; additional slots only need normal-vision ΔE , and weaker pairs are listed in validation.md. Contrast is WCAG against the chart surface; "under :1" counts colours below that on the chart, raised or dialog surface ( is the accepted floor on the last two). Pairs under L* look alike in greyscale or print and rely on markers. Order by hue is per mode and starts at red; colours with almost no chroma come last.
